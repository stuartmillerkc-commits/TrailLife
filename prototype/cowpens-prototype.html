    Morgan's plan as a patrol order

    Reconstructed from the Army staff ride guide's account of Morgan's plan. Not a historical document.

    Situation
    Tarleton is chasing us with about 1,100 men. He's aggressive and always attacks head-on. His men have marched since 3 a.m.
    Mission
    Defeat Tarleton's force here at the Cowpens.
    Commander's intent
    Wear him down in layers. Let him think he's winning. Finish him with the regulars and the hidden cavalry.
    Who does what
    Skirmishers: shoot the officers, then fall back into the militia line.
    Militia: fire your volleys, then withdraw around our left and re-form behind the line.
    Continentals and Virginians: hold the main line on the rise.
    Cavalry: wait out of sight behind our left. Strike wherever we're threatened.
    Signals
    Everyone knows the fall-back route before the fight starts, so a planned retreat never looks like a rout to our own men.

    Sources and accuracy

    Phase times and unit placements follow the timed maps (0700 to 0750) in the U.S. Army's staff ride guide. Positions are approximate. The terrain contours are illustrative, drawn from the guide's description of a gentle rise to about 990 feet, not from a survey.

    • John Moncure, The Cowpens Staff Ride and Battlefield Tour, Combat Studies Institute, 1996 (public domain)
    • American Battlefield Trust: Cowpens
    • Britannica: Battle of Cowpens

    Where sources disagree:

    • Militia volleys: many accounts say two; the staff ride guide records Morgan asking for "three fires," and says the men fired between two and five rounds each.
    • Losses: the staff ride guide gives about 850 British lost (more than 100 killed, more than 200 wounded, more than 500 captured) and 10–12 American killed with 50–60 wounded. The Trust estimates 868 British and 149 American casualties.
    • Strength: estimates run from about 940 to 1,100 Americans and 1,050 to 1,200 British.
