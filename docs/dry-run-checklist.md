# Cowpens dry run (20 minutes, with one or two boys)

The goal is to find what confuses an 11-to-14-year-old before eight of them are watching. Write down what they do, not just what they say.

## Setup (5 min)
- [ ] Open the Cowpens prototype on the screen you'll use at meetings, in projector mode.
- [ ] Lay out the sand table grid (or tape on a table) and the unit markers from the sand table kit.

## Run it (10 min)
- [ ] Start on **Region**. Ask: "Why would Morgan stop here instead of running?" Can they find Cowpens and the rivers without help?
- [ ] Switch to **Terrain**. Can they say where the high ground is?
- [ ] Switch to **Tactical** and play through. At each decision point, have them choose before you press "Show what happened."
- [ ] At each phase, have them move the markers from the phase card's table moves. Does the table match the screen?
- [ ] Turn on **Tarleton's view** at 7:00, then off at 7:30. Do they get why the cavalry surprise worked?

## Watch for
- [ ] Where do they get stuck or ask "what am I looking at"?
- [ ] Can they read the unit labels from where they'd sit?
- [ ] Do the arrows make sense, or do they look like noise?
- [ ] Is any phase boring (no decision, nothing to move)?
- [ ] Do they argue at the decision points? Arguing is good. Silence means the choices are too obvious.
- [ ] How long did it take? The plan budgets about 20 minutes for the battle phases.

## Ask afterward (5 min)
- [ ] "What would you change?"
- [ ] "Would you rather drag the units yourself?" (This tests the game-mode idea.)
- [ ] "What's one thing from Cowpens you could use in capture the flag?"

## Already fixed from my own walkthrough
- At 7:30 and 7:40 the narration and map gave away the answer before the boys chose. Those phases now show a setup paragraph and hold the map until "Show what happened" is pressed.

## Known rough spots to check with real eyes
- Labels crowd at 7:50, when everyone converges.
- On a phone-sized screen the labels are small; check readability on the projector instead.
- Positions are approximate until Claude Code checks them against the staff ride maps (Phase 4a).
