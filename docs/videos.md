# Short videos for leaders and boys

Preview every video before showing it. These were picked for length and source quality, from their descriptions; none has been watched end to end.

| Battle | Video | Length | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cowpens | [American Battlefield Trust: The Battle of Cowpens: Morgan vs. Tarleton](https://battlefields.org/learn/videos/untold/battle-cowpens-morgan-vs-tarleton) | Short | Trust historian Daniel Davis. Best single Cowpens video for the group. |
| Cowpens (context) | [Southern Campaign Animated Map](https://youtu.be/M3jNh3D57gQ) | About 15 min | Animated map, the strategic rung of the ladder. The Trust has a printable question sheet for it. |
| Cowpens | *Cowpens: A Battle Remembered* (NPS park film), via [DCMP](https://dcmp.org/media/7953-cowpens-a-battle-remembered) | 18.5 min | Live-action park film; DCMP may require an account. |
| Pegasus Bridge | [Imperial War Museum: Operation Deadstick, the airborne assault on Pegasus Bridge](https://www.iwm.org.uk/history/second-world-war/d-day/operation-deadstick-pegasus-bridge) | A few minutes | A curator on site at the bridge. The leanest, most accurate option. |
| Westport | The History Guy: "October 23, 1864 Battle of Westport, the 'Gettysburg of the West'" (YouTube, 2023) | About 16 min | Clear narration of the campaign and battle. Search the title on YouTube. |
| Westport (leaders) | [KCUR Up to Date: The Battle of Westport 150 Years Later](https://www.kcur.org/show/up-to-date/2014-06-19/the-battle-of-westport-150-years-later) | Radio segment | A preservationist on the battle and the local sites. Prep for the field trip. |

**What-ifs:** no kid-length video found that walks through alternate outcomes. The Army staff ride guides are built around "what would you do here?" questions, so they're the best source for what-if discussion.
